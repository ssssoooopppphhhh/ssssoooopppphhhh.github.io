<p>My 2024 holiday cards featured the big dipper constellation “painted” in the fluorescent emission of purified iLID protein tethered to a supported membrane. I created the design using 488 nm light directed by a digital micromirror device. To enhance the weak fluorescence of iLID’s natural flavin mononucleotide, this protein was also fused to an mNeonGreen fluorescent protein.</p>
